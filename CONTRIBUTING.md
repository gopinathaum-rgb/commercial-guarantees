# Corrections and Research Contributions

This repository publishes research based on public sources. Contributions should improve the accuracy, source traceability, or clarity of that work.

Useful contributions include:

- corrections supported by a cited public source;
- missing primary-source references;
- distinctions between source statements, findings, and interpretation;
- clarifications about scope or limitations.

Do not submit confidential business records, personal information, or material you are not authorized to share.
