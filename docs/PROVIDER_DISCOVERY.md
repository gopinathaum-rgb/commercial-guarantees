# Provider-side discovery

## Source
The first supply-side source is the official Frappe partner directory:
https://frappe.io/partners/list

The scheduled discovery script extracts India partner-profile links and appends new profiles to the repository's Provider discovery queue issue. It records the source and makes no contact.

## Evidence boundary
- Directory listing proves only that a profile was listed by Frappe when checked.
- It does not independently prove delivery quality, current capacity, current availability, references, willingness to accept a referral fee, or suitability for a particular buyer.
- Provider claims must be checked against recent project evidence and references with permission.
- No provider is called verified for our marketplace until checks are completed and recorded.
- No outreach, introduction, or commission negotiation is automated by this workflow.

## Human verification checklist
1. Confirm the legal entity and official website.
2. Confirm current services and platforms.
3. Obtain two relevant, recent project references with permission to contact.
4. Confirm named delivery capability and current availability.
5. Confirm the provider's target industries, project size and geography.
6. Ask whether they accept disclosed referral introductions and agree to written terms.
7. Record conflicts, exclusions and evidence expiry dates.
