# Company State Visuals

These visuals show **what has changed in the company's understanding or economic position**. They are not decoration or vanity metrics.

## Economic transition

```
COMMITMENT
    ↓
COMPLETION CONDITION
    ↓
EVIDENCE
    ↓
RECOGNITION / ACCEPTANCE
    ↓
ECONOMIC STATE
    ↓
PAYMENT / HANDOVER / RELEASE / OTHER CONSEQUENCE
```

At every transition:

> **What evidence allows the next economic state to be recognized?**

This is the primary visual model because it represents the commercial mechanism we are actually investigating.

## What money means here

Money is shown in context, not as a vanity scoreboard.

- **₹25 crore** — documented value of the J&K Bank × Dynacons engagement used as research context; **not Syzygy revenue**.
- **₹14.44 crore** — amount found due in the ASCON-IV/Ishan Infotech court record; **not Syzygy revenue**.
- **₹55,244.31 crore** — aggregate amount involved in MSE delayed-payment applications through 31 Dec 2025; macro evidence of economic friction, **not Syzygy market size or revenue**.

These numbers answer:

> **How consequential is the economic state we are studying?**

They do not answer:

> **How successful is Syzygy?**

## What success means

Until a customer pays, the most meaningful company-success visual is evidence progression:

**problem observed → situation reconstructed → person engaged → paid transaction → measurable economic effect → repeat**

GitHub commits, page views, research-note count, or prospect count do not move this ladder by themselves.

## Meaningful-change rule

When company state changes materially, update three things together:

1. **Narrative** — what we now believe.
2. **Economic visual** — what economic mechanism or amount became better understood.
3. **Evidence visual** — what company state has actually been established.

Do not update a visual merely because a software commit occurred.

The visual should answer at a glance:

> **What did we learn? What changed economically? What did the market actually give us?**
