# First End-to-End Situation Record — WAPCOS × CYFUTURE

Date: 2026-09-25
Status: Research artifact / founder validation

## 1. Situation

WAPCOS engaged CYFUTURE for an ERP implementation. By April–May 2026, the relationship had become materially disputed around performance, termination, access/data handover, restoration of services, payments/claims, and dispute resolution.

Bounded question:

What aspects of implementation/performance and resulting economic entitlement or consequence can actually be established from the available evidence, and what remains unresolved?

This is deliberately narrower than asking whether either party was right.

## 2. Organizations and relationship

- WAPCOS Limited — ERP procuring/customer organization.
- CYFUTURE India Private Limited — ERP implementation/service provider.
- The 22 May 2026 Delhi High Court order records an RFP dated 9 Feb 2023 and a contract award dated 2 Mar 2023 through GeM.

## 3. Actor / role resolution

The court record names:
- Arun Arora — GM, IT, WAPCOS.
- Shambhu Azad — C.E.D., WAPCOS.
- Virendra Dhawan — Deputy C.E., WAPCOS.
- Justice (Retd.) J.R. Midha — appointed Sole Arbitrator.

The record establishes organizational/role association, but does not by itself establish that any named person held decision authority for a particular milestone, acceptance decision, payment decision, or technical determination.

Therefore: named person does not equal decision authority without further evidence.

## 4. Commercial relationship

Established:
- RFP issued 9 Feb 2023.
- Contract awarded 2 Mar 2023 through GeM.
- The dispute concerns implementation/service obligations.
- WAPCOS relied on contractual provisions concerning termination, handover and dispute resolution.
- CYFUTURE disputed aspects of the governing contractual framework and relied on the General Terms and Conditions and dispute-resolution mechanism.

The 22 May order expressly left rights, contentions, claims/counterclaims and merits open for arbitration.

Therefore:
contract exists ≠ all contractual terms are undisputed
and
termination ≠ adjudicated breach.

## 5. Event chronology

### 09 Feb 2023
WAPCOS issued an RFP for ERP implementation.

### 02 Mar 2023
CYFUTURE was awarded the contract through GeM.

### 16 Apr 2026
WAPCOS issued a termination order, according to the 22 Apr and 22 May court records.

### 17 Apr 2026
The 22 Apr order records invocation of the Performance Bank Guarantee and initiation/proposal of blacklisting proceedings.

### 22 Apr 2026
The Delhi High Court directed immediate restoration steps, cooperation with WAPCOS technical personnel, handover of passwords/passcodes and data, and restoration within an outer limit of 12 hours.

The court also directed the parties toward the contractual dispute-resolution process and recorded that issues including payments and restoration of the contract were to be considered.

### 22 May 2026
The court recorded competing positions:
- WAPCOS said access/data remained affected by glitches.
- CYFUTURE said services had been restored and support was being provided.
- The parties disputed the contractual mechanism and governing terms.

The court appointed Justice (Retd.) J.R. Midha as Sole Arbitrator and expressly left merits and claims/counterclaims open.

## 7. Claims

### C1 — Contractual relationship existed
Status: established at the level of the court record.
Evidence: O1.

### C2 — WAPCOS considered CYFUTURE performance sufficiently problematic to issue termination
Status: established as an action/position of WAPCOS, not as proof of breach.
Evidence: O2 + O3.

### C3 — CYFUTURE asserted restoration/support
Status: party response.
Evidence: O4.

### C4 — Services/data access became a live operational issue requiring interim judicial directions
Status: established.
Evidence: O5.

### C5 — Final determination of breach, entitlement, or monetary liability had been reached by 22 May 2026
Status: not established.
Evidence: O7.

The last item is important: absence of adjudication is part of the situation state.

## 8. Economic state

The record contains at least three economic objects that must not be merged.

### A. WAPCOS asserted economic claim
Approx. Rs. 20 crore.
State: CLAIMED.
Not established as DUE or SETTLED.

### B. CYFUTURE asserted economic claim
Approx. Rs. 5.5 crore.
State: CLAIMED.
Not established as DUE or SETTLED.

### C. Performance Bank Guarantee
The 22 Apr order records invocation of the Performance Bank Guarantee.
State: INVOKED / CONTESTED.
Not equivalent to an adjudicated finding that the underlying breach was established.

Economic conclusion:

The available record establishes competing economic claims and a guarantee-related consequence, but does not establish the final economic entitlement or liability of either party as of 22 May 2026.

This demonstrates:
CLAIMED ≠ DUE ≠ SETTLED
and
GUARANTEE CONSEQUENCE ≠ MERITS DETERMINATION.

## 9. Multiple simultaneous states

The situation cannot be represented as one boolean such as implementation_failed.

At minimum:

| Dimension | Current state |
|---|---|
| Operational | Services/restoration were disputed; court ordered restoration and later recorded competing accounts |
| Contractual | Contract existed; termination and governing contractual mechanism were disputed |
| Technical/data | Access, passwords/passcodes, backups/data and glitches were material |
| Procedural/legal | Interim court protection followed by referral to arbitration |
| Economic | Competing monetary claims existed; PBG invocation was recorded; final entitlement remained unresolved |

These states are related, but not interchangeable.

## 10. What is established?

As of the 22 May 2026 order, the following are supportable:

1. A WAPCOS ERP procurement/contract with CYFUTURE existed.
2. The relationship had reached a material dispute.
3. WAPCOS issued a termination order.
4. Service restoration and data/control handover became subject to interim judicial directions.
5. Both parties asserted monetary positions.
6. A Performance Bank Guarantee had been invoked according to the 22 Apr order.
7. The dispute was referred to arbitration through appointment of a Sole Arbitrator.
8. The court did not determine the merits of the parties' claims.

## 11. What remains unresolved?

The evidence boundary does not establish:
- the complete contractual milestone/acceptance schedule relevant to the disputed economic claims;
- which specific implementation milestones were contractually satisfied;
- whether particular acceptance conditions were met;
- whether alleged technical deficiencies constituted contractual breach;
- the final validity/effect of termination;
- the final amount, if any, due to either party;
- the final economic effect of the PBG invocation;
- whether operational restoration constituted contractual completion;
- whether any later technical state should be treated as contractual realization.

These are actual uncertainty boundaries, not failures to manufacture a conclusion.

## 12. AUM boundary

The situation exposes a possible future bounded commitment:

A specific implementation milestone is complete if and only if a predefined, independently verifiable evidence package establishes the agreed completion conditions by the agreed deadline.

Such an evidence package would need to distinguish:
- contractual completion condition;
- authorized execution record;
- deployment/production evidence;
- mandatory acceptance-test results;
- acceptance authority;
- timestamp;
- independently verifiable record;
- economic consequence.

This is not a proposal to add an AUM primitive yet. The situation only tells us what a bounded commitment would eventually have to make determinable.

## 13. Recon → Evidence OS → Economic State → AUM

| Layer | Demonstrated function |
|---|---|
| Recon | Find and reconstruct a consequential commercial situation |
| Evidence OS | Separate source, observation, claim, stance, uncertainty and historical change |
| Economic State | Keep competing claims, guarantee consequences and final entitlement distinct |
| AUM | Define future commitment conditions so realization can be independently determined |
| Guarantee / Settlement | Apply bounded realization to an economic consequence |

## 14. Where the current system breaks

### Break 1 — Situation identity is implicit
Recon v0.1 can represent observations and claims, but the broader situation identity is not yet a first-class operating artifact.

### Break 2 — Actor resolution is outside the core reconstruction chain
Organizations and named people can be researched manually, but v0.1 does not provide a disciplined situation-linked actor/role record.

### Break 3 — Observation surfaces are not explicit
A legal record, company page, practitioner profile, and public discussion can all be sources, but their role as reusable observation channels is not yet represented.

### Break 4 — Economic state is a reasoning layer, not a reusable situation component
This case shows why a simple claim/status field is insufficient. It does not yet justify a large economic ontology.

### Break 5 — Update semantics are not first-class
The company needs to record dispute → interim restoration direction → competing restoration accounts → arbitration without rewriting earlier observations.

## 15. Minimum v0.2 implication

Do not build a large schema.

The minimum new operating object should be a SituationRecord with only these conceptual sections:

1. identity
2. organizations
3. actors/roles
4. commercial relationship
5. event chronology
6. observation surfaces
7. observations
8. claims
9. disagreements
10. economic-state snapshot
11. unresolved questions
12. updates
13. traceability

The object should reference existing Evidence OS concepts wherever possible rather than duplicate them.

v0.2 should not yet own:
- final economic adjudication;
- autonomous identity inference;
- prospect scoring;
- commitment underwriting;
- guarantee issuance;
- settlement;
- broad crawling.

## 16. Validation result

This first end-to-end record passes the strategic test:

Recon is not merely a lead-generation layer.

It exposes the boundary where uncertain real-world events become economically consequential and where a bounded commitment could eventually make that uncertainty determinable.

The immediate engineering question is therefore not "How do we build more Recon?"

It is:

"What is the smallest reusable SituationRecord that lets the company preserve this chain without losing evidence provenance, actor context, economic-state distinctions, or unresolved uncertainty?"

That is the next implementation boundary.
