# Research Topics

This is the practitioner-facing research surface for **Can You Prove It?**

The work is intentionally narrow. We are not trying to publish a general theory of contracts or enterprise delivery. We are examining a recurring factual question:

> **When a consequential commercial commitment is disputed, can the parties establish what happened from evidence that was clear enough in advance?**

This repository is currently testing a commercial entry point for Recon. That entry point is not the definition of Recon: see [Recon Scope and Purpose](recon-scope.md) for the broader reconstruction capability and its boundaries.

## What we want from practitioners

The most useful contribution is not another abstract opinion.

It is a **real observation**, preferably one that can be described without confidential information:

- a disputed milestone;
- a redacted acceptance condition;
- evidence that settled a disagreement;
- evidence that failed to settle it;
- a commercial consequence that depended on the determination;
- an upcoming commitment where ambiguity is still visible.

### Share a case

If you have something real, start with the [private commitment audit](can-you-prove-it.md).

**Do not publish confidential contract or customer information in a public discussion.**

## Research boundary

This repository is currently testing the commercial problem. It is not claiming that the mechanism is commercially validated.

The rule is simple:

> **Build evidence before expansion.**

A new topic or feature should earn its place through observed practitioner evidence.
