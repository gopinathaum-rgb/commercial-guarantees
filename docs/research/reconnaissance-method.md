# Commercial Reconnaissance Method

This research surface may be extended to study changing commercial situations,
but the experiment remains narrow.

## Recognition cycle

**Observation → State → Change → Trajectory → Commercial relevance → Human decision**

### Observation
What did a public source expose, and when?

### State
What condition appears to exist at that point in time?

### Change
What is different from an earlier supported state?

### Trajectory
Across multiple supported changes, what direction does the situation appear
to be taking?

Trajectory language is descriptive, not predictive. A trajectory may be:

- stable
- improving
- deteriorating
- transitioning
- uncertain

### Commercial relevance
Why might the observed movement matter to a consequential commitment,
acceptance condition, milestone, payment, or other commercial obligation?

This is an investigation question, not a conclusion.

### Human decision
Recon may surface a situation, identify relevant people and conversation
surfaces, and present the evidence chain. A human decides whether and how to
engage.

## Evidence discipline

Recon should never collapse:

- source statement into fact;
- multiple mentions into independent verification;
- correlation into causation;
- trajectory into prediction;
- commercial relevance into customer need.

The purpose is to discover **where reality is moving**, then investigate whether
that movement creates a real commercial problem.

> **The engine should find situations worth investigating, not manufacture prospects.**
