## 1. Decision at a glance

The original bid copy reviewed indicates a ₹92.5 lakh estimated procurement, ₹1.85 lakh EMD, 5% ePBG for 36 months, three-year average annual turnover requirement of ₹3.7 crore, seven years of similar-service experience, and additional recent-project thresholds. The requirements create meaningful qualification, bid-security and delivery exposure.

The public Frappe profiles for New Indictrans and Software@Work show relevant ERPNext/manufacturing experience claims and sizeable partner operations. Those profiles do **not** establish the bidder's audited turnover, qualifying government/PSU contracts, project values and completion status, or compliance with the tender's exact criteria.

The full tender package is not yet reconciled. ICMAI's official listing identifies the original bid, pre-bid meeting details, corrigendum #2 (17 November) and corrigendum #3 (20 November). The contents of those latter documents and buyer-added ATC / detailed attachments have not been inspected in this review.

**Therefore no provider can be recommended as qualified, and no final pursue / do-not-pursue decision can responsibly be issued.**

## 4. Provider evidence screen

This is a preliminary public-profile screen, not provider verification, qualification confirmation or endorsement.

### Candidate A — New Indictrans Technologies Pvt Ltd

**Public evidence observed:** Frappe's partner profile currently describes New Indictrans as a Gold partner, founded 17 years ago, with 75+ Frappe Cloud sites and 75+ team size. It lists manufacturing, electronics manufacturing, distribution, retail, engineering and supply-chain-related sectors.

**What this supports:** A plausible reason to request a formal capability and tender-evidence pack.

**Assessment:** Not proven eligible. First-priority candidate for documentary pre-screen only if and when the full tender package and permitted provider participation route are established.

## 5. Material exposures to resolve

1. **Qualification failure:** turnover and experience requirements may make the opportunity inaccessible to an otherwise capable ERPNext implementer.
2. **Bid security:** ₹1.85 lakh EMD and 5% ePBG for 36 months create financial and banking capacity demands.
3. **Project-reference mismatch:** the bidder must prove the exact customer class, service similarity, project value, completion and relevant period. General implementation counts are insufficient.
4. **Scope and acceptance exposure:** customized ERP across membership, studies, examinations and courses, plus cloud hosting and post-implementation support, needs detailed scope, integration, migration, testing, acceptance and resource analysis.
5. **Payment and penalty exposure:** detailed terms remain uninspected; no margin or cash-flow judgement is possible.
6. **Contractual / procurement restrictions:** buyer-added ATC and amendments may affect consortium, subcontracting, agency, conflict and intermediary structures. These are not yet reviewed.
7. **Opportunity cost:** provider bid-team capacity and delivery commitments are unknown.

## 7. Demonstration conclusion

**Current decision: HOLD — INSUFFICIENT SOURCE PACKAGE.**

This is a useful demonstration of the service because it shows the difference between:
- a tender that appears attractive by headline value;
- a provider that appears capable from its public profile;

## Appendix: evidence classification used

- **Source fact:** directly stated in an identified source.
- **Provider claim:** statement published by the provider or its directory profile.
- **Independently verified fact:** corroborated by a source independent of the provider, within the scope described.
- **Inference:** reasoned conclusion drawn from identified evidence; not itself a source fact.
- **Unknown:** not evidenced or not inspectable.
- **Contradicted:** available evidence conflicts with the requirement or another source.

A missing document or missing provider evidence is recorded as **unknown**, not assumed to be compliant or non-compliant.
