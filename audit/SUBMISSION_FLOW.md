# Commitment Audit — Submission Flow

The submission should take approximately five minutes.

## 1. Commitment type

Select one:

- Enterprise software implementation
- Supplier delivery
- Construction
- Manufacturing
- Professional/service delivery
- Other

## 2. The commitment

Prompt:

> **What does the commitment say?**

Allow the participant to paste the relevant text or provide a redacted document/image.

Instruction:

> Remove names, prices, customer information, credentials, and other confidential information before submitting. Submit only material you are authorized to share.

## 3. Context

Ask only:

**Who makes the commitment?**

- Provider / Vendor
- Supplier
- Contractor
- Internal team
- Other

**Who relies on it?**

- Customer / Buyer
- Internal team
- Other

**Does completion have a commercial consequence?**

- Payment
- Payment retention
- Penalty
- SLA consequence
- Contractual acceptance
- Other
- Not sure

## 4. Incident history

Ask:

> **Has this commitment ever been disputed, delayed, or difficult to establish?**

- Yes
- No
- Not sure

If yes:

> Briefly describe what happened.

This field is especially important because a specific past incident is stronger evidence than a hypothetical reaction.

## 5. Contact

- Name
- Work email
- Company

Optional:

> I would like to discuss the audit after it is completed.

## Submission boundary

Do not ask for a meeting before receiving the commitment.

Do not require an account.

Do not ask whether the participant would buy AUM.

Do not ask for a hypothetical future commitment when a real one is available.

## Privacy

The service should be operated with a manual redaction-first process. Do not request confidential information that is unnecessary for the audit. Do not publish submitted artifacts or audit results without explicit permission.
