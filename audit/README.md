# Can You Prove It? — Commercial Commitment Audit

## Purpose

**Can You Prove It?** is a small research service from Syzygy Dynamics.

A participant submits one real commercial commitment. We manually test whether its completion can be established from defined evidence and verification rules.

The service is deliberately narrow:

> **One real commitment → one audit → one page → one follow-up decision.**

This is a commercial-validation experiment, not a product launch.

## Customer promise

> **Your contract says the milestone is complete. Can you actually prove it?**

Submit one redacted commitment — a milestone, acceptance condition, delivery obligation, go-live requirement, supplier commitment, or similar obligation.

We return a short audit mapping:

**Commitment → Completion condition → Evidence → Verification → Consequence → Reconstruction**

## What the participant receives

A one-page Commercial Commitment Audit covering:

- what the commitment actually requires;
- what evidence would establish completion;
- where material ambiguity exists;
- who or what has verification authority;
- what happens if evidence is inconclusive; and
- whether an independent reviewer could reconstruct the completion decision later.

The audit is analytical research, not legal advice and not a determination of contractual rights.

## What we are learning

The service is designed to test one commercial question:

> **Will a real organization provide a consequential commercial commitment to analyze, and after seeing the analysis, will it want to run the method on a real upcoming commitment?**

The strongest signal is not a compliment or a form submission. It is access to a real upcoming commitment and willingness to run the method on it.

## MVP operating model

```
Landing page
    ↓
Submission
    ↓
Manual audit
    ↓
One-page result
    ↓
"Would this have changed a real event?"
    ↓
Live commitment
```

No automated contract analysis, scoring, dashboard, SaaS workflow, or pricing is part of this MVP.
