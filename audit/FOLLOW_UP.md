# Commitment Audit — Conversion Step

The audit should not end with a product pitch.

The first follow-up question is:

> **If this commitment had been structured differently before execution, would it have changed anything that actually happened?**

Record the answer.

If the answer is **yes** or **possibly**, ask:

> **What upcoming commitment would you want to make easier to establish?**

Then determine:

1. What is being promised?
2. When must it happen?
3. What constitutes completion?
4. What evidence will exist?
5. Who verifies it?
6. What happens if it does not happen?
7. Who is economically exposed?
8. Who needs to agree to the mechanism?

## Live-commitment conversion

If there is a real upcoming milestone:

> **Would you be willing to run this structure on the next real milestone?**

A positive answer is the first meaningful pilot signal.

The progression is:

```
Audit
  ↓
Real upcoming commitment
  ↓
Commitment design
  ↓
Execution
  ↓
Evidence
  ↓
Verification
  ↓
Commercial consequence
```

Only after a live commitment has been successfully structured should the conversation move toward a commercial guarantee.

## Do not use

- "Would you buy AUM?"
- "Would you use this?"
- "Do you like the idea?"
- generic product demos
- pricing before the problem and workflow are established

The objective is behavioral evidence, not enthusiasm.
