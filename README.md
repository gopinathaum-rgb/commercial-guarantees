# Can You Prove It? — Public Research

This repository publishes research on reconstructing consequential commercial commitments from documentary evidence. It focuses on the relationship between performance, acceptance, recognition, payment, and other contractual outcomes.

## Research question

When a commercial commitment depends on a real-world condition, what can the available record establish, what remains unresolved, and how does the evidence relate to the decision or outcome?

## Repository scope

Only public-source research, research methods, and supporting non-sensitive materials are published here. Private company operations and commercial records are maintained separately. Do not submit confidential or personal information through public issues.
