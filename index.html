Consequential commitments often have a deadline and a consequence, but the parties may not have made the transition from performance to recognition sufficiently explicit.

“Production deployment will be completed by October 31.”

What counts as complete?

Deployment alone? Acceptance tests? Production verification? Customer sign-off?

What evidence proves it?

Which records, timestamps, test results or observations establish the condition?

Who decides?

Who has the contractual or operational authority to recognize completion?

What moves economically?

Does recognition release payment, end a liability, trigger a guarantee, or change another obligation?
